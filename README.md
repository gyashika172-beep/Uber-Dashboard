# Uber-Dashboard
# 🚕 Uber Bike Ride Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

📊 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze Uber Bike ride performance across bookings, revenue, distance, pickup locations, vehicle categories, and customer/driver ratings. The dashboard transforms raw Kaggle data into actionable business insights using Power Query, DAX, data modeling, and interactive visualizations.

# Business Problems

The dashboard addresses key business questions:

* How many bookings are completed vs. cancelled or incomplete?
* What are the monthly and quarterly booking trends?
* How is revenue performing over time?
* Which pickup locations have the highest booking demand?
* What is the average distance covered per ride?
* How do customer and driver ratings compare?
* Which ride/vehicle categories contribute to overall performance?
* Where are the opportunities to reduce booking losses and improve operations?

# 🎯 Goal of the Dashboard

The main goal is to provide a centralized and interactive view of Uber Bike operations and performance.

The dashboard helps stakeholders:

* Monitor important business KPIs
* Identify booking and revenue trends
* Understand demand across pickup locations
* Evaluate customer and driver satisfaction
* Compare ride/vehicle categories
* Identify operational improvement opportunities
* Make faster, data-driven business decisions

# Tech Stack

* Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization
* DAX – Measures, KPIs, calculations and aggregations
* Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
* Data Modeling – Relationships and analytical model
* Data Source – Kaggle
* File Format – CSV
* Visualizations – KPI Cards, Donut Charts, Bar Charts, Line/Area Charts, Buttons and Interactive Navigation

# Key Features & Visuals

# KPI Cards

The dashboard provides an instant overview of major business metrics:

* 14K Completed Bookings
* 8K Lost Bookings
* 378,666 Total Distance
* 24.65 Average Distance
* ₹7.8M Revenue

# Booking Trend Analysis

The booking trend visual tracks completed bookings by month, helping identify demand fluctuations throughout the year.

The Month / Quarter toggle allows users to switch between monthly and quarterly analysis.

# Revenue Analysis

The revenue section displays overall revenue along with monthly revenue performance, making it easier to identify stronger and weaker revenue periods.

# Booking Status Analysis

Donut charts provide a visual breakdown of:

* Completed Bookings
* Cancelled Bookings
* Incomplete Bookings

This helps identify potential booking losses and operational gaps.

# Pickup Location Analysis

The dashboard highlights the top pickup locations based on booking volume.

Tagore Garden is shown as the top pickup location with 102 bookings.

# Customer & Driver Ratings

The dashboard tracks service quality through:

* 4.40 Average Customer Rating
* 4.23 Average Driver Rating

These metrics provide visibility into customer and driver satisfaction.

# Vehicle/Ride Category Analysis

Interactive vehicle/ride category navigation allows users to explore performance across different ride categories and understand their contribution to overall operations.

# 💡 Key Insights

* 14K completed bookings compared with 8K lost bookings highlights an opportunity to investigate cancellations and incomplete rides.
* Approximately ₹7.8M revenue provides a high-level view of financial performance.
* Monthly booking trends reveal fluctuations in demand throughout the year.
* Tagore Garden is the top pickup location with 102 bookings, indicating relatively high demand.
* Customer rating of 4.40 and driver rating of 4.23 indicate strong overall service ratings within the analyzed dataset.
* An average ride distance of 24.65 provides insight into typical trip characteristics.

# 🚀 Business Impact

* Helps identify high-demand locations for better driver allocation.
* Highlights lost booking opportunities and areas requiring operational attention.
* Provides better visibility into revenue trends and performance.
* Helps monitor customer and driver satisfaction.
* Reduces dependency on manual reporting through automated calculations and dashboards.
